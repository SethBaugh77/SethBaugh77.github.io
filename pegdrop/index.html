Peg Drop

Drop a ball through pegs, land in scoring slots, and collect 50 unique balls. Simple, satisfying, and always one more drop away from your next unlock.

Peg Drop Peg Drop is a satisfying Plinko-style game where you drop a ball through a field of pegs and watch it bounce into scoring slots. Slide to aim, tap to drop, and collect coins based on where the ball lands. Use your coins to unlock 50 unique collectible balls across 10 themed sets — from sports balls and space planets to glowing neon orbs and legendary rarities. Unlock new board layouts that remix the peg patterns and change how the ball bounces. Simple to play, endlessly fun to watch, and always one more drop away from your next unlock.
