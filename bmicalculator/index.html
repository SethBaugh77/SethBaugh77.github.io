BMI Tracker+

Fast, clean BMI tracking with goal BMI and target weight insights.

BMI Tracker+ is a simple, modern BMI calculator designed to give you useful health insights in seconds. Enter your height and weight to instantly see your BMI, category, and helpful visuals that make the result easy to understand.

Includes a Goal BMI slider so you can see the target weight for your height, plus a healthy weight range for quick reference. Supports Imperial (feet/inches or inches) and Metric units.

No accounts. No clutter. Just the info you need. 💪📈
