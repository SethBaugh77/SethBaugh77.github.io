Dice Roll Pro 3D

Roll realistic 3D dice with satisfying physics. Unlock D4 through D20, collect premium skins, and roll up to five at once. Your tabletop companion.

Dice Roll Pro 3D Dice Roll Pro 3D is a sleek, physics-based dice app for tabletop gamers, board game nights, and quick decisions. Flick to roll realistic 3D dice with satisfying physics and haptic feedback. Start with a standard D6 and unlock the full set — D4, D8, D10, D12, and D20 — by watching a short ad. Roll up to five dice at once, tap to hold and re-roll the rest, and view your roll history. Unlock premium dice skins like metal, wood, crystal, and neon with rewarded ads or go Pro. Clean, fast, no text needed — just shake or tap and roll.
